# Fixed-design computational extension

Written before running the extension on 2026-10-04, after inspecting the original benchmark. This is a local analysis record, not an external preregistration or a blinded prospective protocol. No outcome-driven choice of seeds, thresholds, features or models is permitted. All implemented analyses, including failures and uninformative diagnostics, will be retained.

1. Use all 45 valid unique original training records; keep ACT-18 visible as invalid. Do not repair its chemistry by guessing. Original inputs remain unchanged.
2. Preserve the original six models and hyperparameters. Add constant-score, fingerprint-logistic and kNN baselines; fixed single-descriptor and leave-one-descriptor-out logistic ablations; leave-one-component-out fusion. No tuning. Seeds 42–46, not best-seed selection.
3. Exact-scaffold outer folds are unchanged. Add single-linkage similarity components at Morgan Tanimoto >=0.70 with grouped folds as a chemical-neighbourhood stress test. If infeasible, report infeasibility, not a relaxed threshold chosen for scores.
4. A deterministic 1:1 nearest-property subset (seven whole-cohort standardized descriptors, positives in ID order, negatives without replacement) is an exploratory cohort-design sensitivity check, NOT an independent property-controlled test. Report balance before/after, matching distances, and whether any positive has a negative within 0.5 original-cohort SD in every descriptor. Do not imply nearest matching guarantees balance or removes confounding. Cohort-design normalization uses the full dataset; predictive feature preprocessing uses outer training data only.
5. Report complete OOF rows/assignments, nearest-training and nearest-active similarity with fixed bins [0,.3), [.3,.5), [.5,.7), [.7,.85), [.85,1], class counts, descriptors/domain shifts, AP and trapezoidal PR-AUC separately, ROC-AUC, MCC, F1, balanced accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, precision, NPV, Brier score and five equal-width reliability bins. Threshold .5 is arbitrary, not optimized. Similarity/fusion scores are not calibrated activity probabilities.
6. Fixed-OOF exact-scaffold bootstrap (1,000 draws) and paired AUC differences quantify conditional resampling only, excluding retraining uncertainty. Keep five-seed ranges separate. No confirmatory significance tests for this exploratory extension.
7. Split-conformal RF stress test: group-disjoint proper-training and calibration scaffolds inside each outer training fold; retain calibration class counts, minimum p-value resolution, per-record class p-values and sets at alpha .1/.2. Report classwise coverage and efficiency, not a coverage guarantee under grouped distribution shift or uncertain labels. Insufficient classes must fail visibly, not be silently skipped.
8. Fixed-model retrospective transfer: fit the existing six models to the original 45 records, evaluate all measured Balikci source records after excluding exact/standardized parent/tautomer training matches. Retain all 7 numeric IC50 and 5 inactive (>50 uM) records in the accounting; 11 untested never become negatives. Numeric IC50 <50uM is detectable biochemical inhibition, NOT lead potency. Fixed 1/10/50uM label sensitivities will all be reported; measured values above lower cutoffs are threshold negatives, not experimentally inactive. This single previously inspected publication is NOT a blind or independent prospective test; no hyperparameter tuning on it.
9. Chemical identity: canonical isomeric SMILES, standard InChI/InChIKey, fragment/charge status, standardized parent and canonical tautomer keys as alternative identity flags only. Preserve original graphs and stereochemistry. No biological label transfer from alerts or normalization. Candidate tables separate model-score ordering, identity/similarity novelty (not patent novelty) and conditional experimental prioritization.
10. Do not add docking/MD merely for appearance. Research public assays and receptor structures separately, then write a testable selectivity/mechanism roadmap grounded in their evidence.
