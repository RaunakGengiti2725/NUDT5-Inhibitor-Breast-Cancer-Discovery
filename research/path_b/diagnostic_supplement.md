# Diagnostic supplement: source-label controls and provenance limits

Author-review material accompanying the structural pharmacology reanalysis. No new biological experiment was performed. The calculations in this supplement describe the supplied label task and already-inspected sources. They do not establish target recognition, activity probabilities, selectivity, efficacy or chemical novelty. Frozen numerical inputs and their historical manifests are preserved. Regeneration has its own execution record.

## S1. Historical scope and identity

The original release under examination is 8c2a1b6990df1e140e15ab5a0eabea69b70eee14. The audited manuscript/reanalysis baseline is 40b9b0708d888a015abe5043bb273c3c6ee601ae. They are different versions. The original release contains five tracked files; the archived Zenodo payload contains those same files. The upstream source handoff verified the original CSV hashes against the author-supplied fingerprints. The DataPort linkage is retained as archive-reported provenance, not a new inspection of its restricted PDFs. Those PDFs were not inspected here.

The author has confirmed that the historical 18,412-compound library is unavailable and is not to be recreated. Library-derived counts, cascade enrichment, docking/MM-GBSA results and candidate-discovery conclusions are withdrawn from the current manuscript. Other missing artifacts include the claimed proxy set, larger decoy collection, receptor preparation, poses, run logs and original assay-level label provenance. Missing release artifacts do not prove that no other historical work ever happened. The subsequently curated published-assay ledgers are not the missing original activity ledger.

The original CSV has 46 rows, 20 positive labels and 26 decoys. ACT-18 fails chemical parsing and remains quarantined without an invented replacement. Forty-five valid unique records remain, 19 with positive labels. Most positive labels are unauthenticated: only ACT-19 and ACT-20 have the audited source assay mapping. A source annotation is not a measured-label provenance chain. Decoys are unmatched, unassayed presumed negatives. The historical DOCX explicitly states strict absolute molecular-weight difference <50, absolute cLogP difference <1.0 and Tanimoto <0.3 criteria. The original code lacks an executed joint matching check and per-decoy matching provenance. This corrects the earlier false denial of those written criteria; it does not authenticate matching. Applying all three written tolerances jointly to the retained valid graphs, only 9 of 26 decoys satisfy them against any valid source-positive, and the only positives ever matched are ACT-19 and ACT-20. The retained source-positive and decoy molecular-weight ranges do not overlap, 303.325–491.339 against 206.285–291.734; raw MW alone separates the labels with AUC 1.000, and raw HBA and TPSA each give 0.998. The label task is therefore separable by size and polarity before any model is fitted.

NC5-02 is ACT-19 and published compound 11 in Balikci et al. It is known chemistry. The bounded identity comparison covers all candidates and the recorded original-training, author-CSV, database, PubChem, measured-ledger and authenticated-CCD pools. Non-overlap in these finite pools does not establish chemical novelty, freedom to operate or activity. Canonical-parent-tautomer equivalence is a screening key, not physical sample identity, stereochemical equivalence or assay interchangeability. PubChem CID 22346757 agrees with NC5-02 only after canonical-parent-tautomer normalization in this check, despite a shared standard InChIKey. Database repeats are not independent experiments.

The ACT-01 graph is not authenticated TH5427; ACT-02 is not authenticated TH1713. The source checks distinguish TH5427/9CH/5NWH, TH1713/958/5NQR and compound 9/W0O/8RIY/8OTV by graph and identifiers. The conflicting TH5427 synonym in CCD 958 is retained as a conflict. Original files are not repaired silently. The separate authenticated-reference sensitivity replaces only ACT-01/02 for its own recorded scenario, recomputes scaffolds/folds and preserves all original-graph results. Neither scenario supplies newly authenticated labels for the rest of the dataset.

## S2. Current implemented diagnostic methods

Structurally parseable records receive default RDKit sanitization, canonical isomeric SMILES and exact Bemis–Murcko scaffolds. Recoverable invalid records retain logical record index, identifier and validation error, not a complete parser transcript. Fatal CSV errors stop processing. Canonical duplicates collapse to recorded representatives; conflicting labels fail closed. This operation does not silently merge salts, protomers, tautomers or stereoisomers.

The representation is Morgan radius 2, 2,048 bits, default bond typing and no chirality. Current RF has 100 trees; scikit-learn GBT has 100 trees of depth 3; RBF-SVM uses C=10. All use fixed specifications. SVM internal probability estimation sees training data only; its outputs are not validated biochemical probabilities. The nearest-active score is maximum training-positive Tanimoto. Equal_mean is the raw mean of RF, GBT, RBF-SVM and nearest-active scores, each with weight 0.25. Property_LR is separate. There is no fused-score min–max normalization, learned transfer weight, proxy-target loss, eight-component consensus, docking term or trained fused-score calibration. The historical benchmark instead min–max normalized its components; the historical candidate loop used a raw mean with named-reference similarity. Neither is equivalent to the current implementation, and neither establishes transfer weighting.

The seven properties are molecular weight (MW), calculated octanol/water partition coefficient (cLogP), topological polar surface area (TPSA), hydrogen-bond donor count (HBD), hydrogen-bond acceptor count (HBA), rotatable-bond count (NRB) and fraction of sp3-hybridized carbon atoms (Fsp3). Logistic models use training-fold standardization. The controls include seven single-property and seven leave-one-property-out logistic fits, descriptor-only logistic regression, fingerprint logistic regression, uniform five-neighbour Tanimoto classification, constant 0.5, fold training prevalence, fingerprint-plus-property RF and four leave-one-consensus-component-out means. They are explanatory controls for label composition, not activity validators.

Unique-molecule stratification permits related analogues across folds. Exact-scaffold grouped five-fold evaluation prevents exact scaffold sharing, not all medicinal-chemistry-series overlap. Positive-series holdout uses two supplied positive-series groups and disjoint decoy partitions, so it has two folds and substantial partition sensitivity. Supplied series names are not independently validated chemical taxonomies. Exact-scaffold grouping does not remove analogue leakage here: 16 of 19 source-positives have Tanimoto >=0.70 to a positive in another fold, and fold 0 holds 11 of 19 positives while the remaining folds hold one to three. Within-fold ROC-AUC is 1.000 for Property_LR in all five folds and for Equal_mean in four of five, against pooled 0.9798 and 0.9312; 87 within-fold comparable pairs exist against 494 pooled pairs. Pooled and within-fold values answer different questions and are reported side by side in the generated annex. Under the two-fold holdout of the supplied positive-series groups, Equal_mean falls to 0.5628 and Nearest_active to 0.3289 while Property_LR is 0.9980. Equal_mean does not exceed Property_LR in the seed-42 scaffold, series-holdout, nearest-property-subset or similarity-component comparisons. The unique-molecule split is an exception (Equal_mean 0.9980, Property_LR 0.9960), as is its authenticated-reference sensitivity (0.9980 versus 0.9939); neither isolates analogue-independent recognition. Equal_mean also exceeds Property_LR in the separate published-source challenge, 1.000 against 0.5625 at the 1 and 10 µM cutoffs, on ten endpoints of which two are threshold-positive; that cohort is an exposed same-paper analogue set and establishes no generalization. A separate fixed Tanimoto >=0.70 connected-component split is different from, and not necessarily harder than, scaffold grouping. Infeasible designs remain explicit; no threshold is changed to obtain a result.

All main-text controls use the single recorded full-valid-set, seed-42, exact-scaffold partition in controls.json. Their row identities, labels, folds and stored score paths are exported. Single-descriptor score arrays are aligned by the stored index, not by presentation order. No in-sample raw-descriptor AUC is substituted for out-of-fold logistic AUC. The constant score gives pooled AUC 0.5. The fold-prevalence baseline varies between folds; pooled AUC can be below 0.5 although its within-fold ordering is constant. Pooled scores mix separately fitted models. This is not evidence of anticorrelated target recognition.

The local extension_design.md records the design. It is not external preregistration or independent proof of pre-outcome timing. All retained seeds 42–46 and fixed specifications are reported; code inspection cannot certify the absence of undisclosed earlier exploration.

## S3. Metrics, resampling and calibration boundaries

ROC-AUC is area under the receiver operating characteristic curve. Average precision and trapezoidal precision–recall area are distinct and separately recorded. Enrichment uses ceil(fraction × N) and records the realized cutoff; with 45 molecules, nominal 1% and 5% select one and three molecules. BEDROC20 uses the finite-list normalized exponential formula and expected contributions for exact ties. A software comparison with RDKit checks arithmetic only. The original BEDROC normalization defect and unsmoothed five-permutation p-value defect are separate implementation errors, not independent biological findings.

Sensitivity, specificity, precision, negative predictive value, Matthews correlation, F1 and balanced accuracy use an arbitrary score threshold of 0.5. Matthews correlation follows scikit-learn's convention and is reported as 0.0000 when its denominator is zero, which is what the annex publishes for every constant-prediction row; that zero is a convention, not an estimated correlation. Precision and negative predictive value remain null when there are no predicted members of the corresponding class; the denominator definition supplies the reason. One-class AUC is undefined and reported separately, not zero. Brier scores and five fixed equal-width score bins describe the frequency of source labels within each bin. controls.calibration_bins implements descriptive expected calibration error (ECE). No calibration model is trained for Equal_mean and no clinical or biochemical probability calibration is claimed. Empty score bins are omitted, not imputed.

Maximum training and training-positive similarity bins are [0,0.3), [0.3,0.5), [0.5,0.7), [0.7,0.85), [0.85,1]. One-class bin or fold AUC is undefined and retained as such. These bins do not establish a validated applicability domain. Uncertainty summaries resample exact-scaffold groups from fixed out-of-fold scores for 1,000 draws. They omit retraining uncertainty; valid and one-class draws are counted. Every such range is a fixed-score group-resampling percentile range, not a population confidence interval: the scores are frozen and no model is refitted inside a draw. The ranges depend on the resampled grouping. In the similarity split, resampling the stored Tanimoto components gives Equal_mean [0.9286, 1.0000] with 17 single-class draws, against [0.9339, 1.0000] with none when exact scaffolds are resampled. Paired Equal_mean-minus-Property_LR intervals use paired resampling of the same scores; the stored full-valid-set paired range is [-0.2464, 0.0000] around a point difference of -0.0486, so it does not separate the two scores. These conditional intervals and repeated-seed ranges are not population confidence intervals or independent experiments.

The stored molecule-label randomization uses 99 refitted permutations and the plus-one formula (exceedances+1)/(B+1), whose floor is 0.01. For B=30 and B=5 the smallest plus-one-corrected Monte Carlo values would be 1/31 and 1/6. These statements do not concern exhaustive exact tests. Correlated chemistry is not biologically exchangeable; the diagnostic cannot validate labels or exclude dataset bias.

The grouped class-conditional conformal diagnostic uses the first of two inner group-disjoint partitions of each outer training fold for proper training and calibration, an RF, nonconformity 1−p, >= ties, plus-one smoothing and strict p>alpha inclusion. Alpha is 0.1 or 0.2. Class-conditional exchangeability is not assured under group shift, related chemistry or unverified labels. These are observed source-label inclusion frequencies; tiny calibration classes can force both-label sets. In the similarity split the class-1 calibration count is 2 in all five outer folds, so the smallest attainable class-1 p-value is 1/3 and class 1 is force-included at alpha 0.1 and 0.2; 43 of 45 and 42 of 45 prediction sets then contain both labels. In the full valid set at alpha 0.1, 22 of 45 molecules lie in folds where both classes are force-included by the minimum p-value alone. The similarity split is therefore a sparse-calibration stress test, and the inclusion frequencies in the annex are observed source-label inclusion, not validated coverage. An absent calibration class yields p=1, not evidence for that class. These are not guarantees of shifted-population coverage or estimates for new inhibitors.

The exploratory property subset standardizes the whole cohort for cohort design only, processes positives in identifier order, greedily selects unused nearest decoys and reports a 0.5-full-cohort-SD all-property caliper. It neither creates new decoys nor guarantees balance, unbiased adjustment or independence. Predictive scaling remains training-only. Matching did not remove the diagnosed imbalance: on the nearest-property subset Property_LR rises to 0.9945 while Equal_mean is 0.9280. The subset therefore demonstrates that the imbalance survives property matching; it is not evidence of adequate balance. Performance cannot identify which causal mixture of properties, analogues or activity-linked features produced the scores.

## S4. Already-inspected source challenges and endpoint boundaries

The current six fixed methods were fit at seed 42 to all 45 valid original-input graphs and scored all 23 Balikci author-CSV structures. These fitted objects differ from the out-of-fold models. Exact, neutral-parent and canonical-parent-tautomer exclusions apply only to challenge eligibility; scored overlaps and untested structures remain in the ledger. Five numeric and five right-censored NUDT5 endpoints remain after the recorded exclusions. Untested means not tested in this source dataset, not never tested anywhere. All fixed cutoffs are retained, including those with one class for which AUC is unavailable.

This is an exposed single-publication challenge with related chemistry, not a blinded holdout or target-wide generalization estimate. Changing cohort, fitting regime and label definition together is not a controlled performance-drop experiment. Source mean classification does not propagate SD; a reported mean near a threshold is not an uncertainty-resolved boundary. Censoring above 50 µM does not supply exact inactive potency, and a threshold-negative at 1 µM is not necessarily inactive. These cutoffs are presented separately from the same-split main-text controls. Cohort composition drives the challenge numbers: of 23 scored source structures, 11 are excluded as untested and 2 as training identity or parent overlap, leaving ten endpoints. At the 1 and 10 µM cutoffs only compounds 1 and 9 are threshold-positive, and at 50 µM five are. Equal_mean, Nearest_active, RF and SVM_RBF all reach AUC 1.000 at every cutoff on that cohort while Property_LR falls to 0.5625 at the tighter cutoffs. These are retrospective diagnostics of the frozen implementation, not tuning-free stress tests of a predictor. Baseline availability differs: the historical source challenge records six methods, not the full original-label ablation panel. No superiority inference across these validations is made.

The probe challenge is one previously known SGC/MSD MRK-952/MRK-952-NC pair. Low maximum training similarity is specific to the radius-2/2,048-bit representation, not a universal chemical-space distance. NC is a weak inhibitor, not an inactive decoy. Species/construct information and the assumed control stereochemistry are unresolved in the producer dossier; figure EC50 and dossier IC50 terminology differ, and the meaning of the reported SPR ± uncertainty is unspecified. Trivial properties and similarity can agree with the source order; a model agreeing on this pair gains no method-specific validation. For symmetric strict random ordering either order has probability 0.5, which is not a calculated p-value and does not cover ties. The models are not potency regressors.

The bounded archived database collection contains repeated records rather than independent experimental validation. PubChem linkage preserves the primary-source/assay-description protocol discrepancy, censored relations and missing endpoint values; omission of a relation in concise data is not an exact value. The inspected ledgers and paired-ratio code keep catalytic IC50, direct-binding KD, cellular engagement EC50, Kinobead apparent affinity, viability and qualitative endpoints distinct. No mixed-endpoint potency outcome is fitted. This does not certify every external primary experiment. Nonenzymatic/scaffolding, depletion, degradation and catalytic inhibition are distinct perturbations. Nguyen is used only for abstract-supported context; full-text, figure and Y74E inspection claims are removed. Access failure does not disprove a publication or any earlier inspection.

## S5. Limitations preserved beyond the main text

This is one small source-label dataset. Its two-series holdout is noisy, labels remain largely unverified and the original decoys are unassayed. The invalid ACT-18 is not corrected by guessing. Identity uses a pinned toolkit and finite source pools, not exhaustive chemical or patent searches. Descriptor agreement is at the stored CSV precision, not unrounded floating-point equality; toolkit-version-dependent acceptor counts need not be transcription errors. PAINS absence and rule-of-five/Veber compliance establish neither selectivity, safety, solubility nor activity. Candidate scores in the original file remain historical assertions, not an efficacy ranking regenerated from the missing library.

There is no source-task transfer-learning study, new library, replacement decoy cohort, docking, molecular dynamics or deep-learning study here. Historical absence of a machine-readable execution/input-hash manifest does not deny existing source annotations. The older code could emit RDKit errors while omitting invalid rows without a retained row audit. The corrected implementation is not guaranteed error-free. Tests establish software behavior, not the correctness of unauthenticated inputs or biological validity.

All crystal copies remain descriptive and nonindependent. Missing or zero-occupancy atoms cannot be inferred from nearby retained atoms. Complete-residue minima and coordinate-error propagation are not estimated; B factors are metadata. The geometry neither excludes other contacts nor gives affinity, energy, causality, homology or selectivity. Local report/map findings are bounded by precomputed model-dependent maps, interpolation and fixed slices. No new biological experiment, omit map, rerefinement, expert 3D review or independent ligand-placement validation was performed. The previously uninterpreted per-atom fields are now reported with their retrieved definitions. EDIA scores the electron-density fit of an individual atom; EDIAm aggregates per-atom EDIA over a residue or group through a power mean, and the wwPDB dictionary states that a value below 0.8 indicates increasing inconsistency with the density and should prompt visual inspection. OPIA is the percentage of atoms in a residue whose EDIA exceeds 0.8, with below 50% indicating that fewer than half the atoms are well supported. Retrieval records and archived pages are in research/path_b/repair/definitions_sources.json; the primary method article was inaccessible (403), so the definitions come from the authors' software documentation and the wwPDB dictionary. For W0O these give EDIAm/OPIA 0.412/16.67 (8RIY AAA 301), 0.410/26.67 (8RIY BBB 301), 0.804/83.33 (8OTV A 301) and 0.728/60.00 (8OTV B 302); for Arg51, 0.199/18.18 in AAA and 0.413/45.45 in BBB. These values are less favourable than the site RSCC and RSR and are reported beside them in main-text Table 1 rather than set aside. By these thresholds the ligand and Arg51 atoms are not uniformly well supported by the deposited density, which further limits any atom-specific interpretation. The missing map-generation version remains recorded in the model-support supplement.
