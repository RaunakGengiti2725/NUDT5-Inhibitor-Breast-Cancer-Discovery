## Editorial changes and limitations disposition

- The research-note article category replaces the unsupported JMGM primary-research framing. No biological evidence was added and no earlier review is represented as cleared.
- The main argument is the distinction between atom-specific observed coordinates and mechanistic interpretation. Catalytic arithmetic and screening controls are explicitly secondary.
- All three main numerical tables remain source-generated. Unfavourable EDIAm/OPIA values and simple baselines are not hidden in a supplement.
- Scientific limitations from the longer revision remain in the main note or expanded supplementary methods/diagnostics. Formatting history and missing declaration requests move to internal author materials; they are not scientific results. The old “guide inaccessible/no cover letter prepared” status is superseded by actual journal-source retrieval and preparation.
- The draft has no fabricated declaration section. Completed declarations can be generated only from actual author statements, with explicit confirmations; an automated flag does not establish that statements are true.
- The principal editorial risk is still limited distinct contribution. The note tests reproducible reporting and interpretation in a narrow published example; it does not settle a biological mechanism or generalize to all screening benchmarks.
- The institution-verified reviewer candidates are provisional. No candidate is characterized as conflict-free, willing, contacted or supportive.
