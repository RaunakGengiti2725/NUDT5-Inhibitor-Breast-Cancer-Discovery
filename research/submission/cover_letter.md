# Cover-letter scientific core

AUTHOR-REVIEW DRAFT. The text below is not a complete submission letter. Required statements about competing interests, author approval and publication/submission history must come from the author. No statement of exclusivity or approval is implied.

Dear Editors,

Please consider “Occupancy-aware reanalysis of deposited NUDT5 and NUDT14 inhibitor complexes” as a Research Note in BMC Research Notes.

The study extends inspection of the compound-9 complexes reported by Balikci and colleagues. Its contribution is an auditable, site-resolved description of all four deposited ligand sites, retaining occupancy, alternate conformers and missingness. At the two NUDT5 sites, the nearest retained Arg51 atoms differ: backbone nitrogen and a fractional-occupancy side-chain carbon. Neither witness establishes a uniform guanidinium interaction or energetic dependence.

We place these observations beside official model-support fields and the source's catalytic endpoints. Whole-ligand agreement measures coexist with less favourable atom-level density summaries. Reported catalytic ratios remain constrained by unequal reaction durations, shared normalization and missing replicate covariance. A separate historical screening example includes constant, prevalence, nearest-neighbour and single-descriptor controls in the main text. Those controls expose source-label separation rather than validate target-specific recognition.

The note reports no new inhibitor, biological experiment or prospective validation. It cannot establish binding energetics, selectivity or therapeutic efficacy. The absent historical screening library is not reconstructed, and dependent discovery claims are removed. Published structures and measurements are explicitly credited; no pooled meta-analysis is performed.

We propose the Research Note category because its criteria include extensions of published work and additional controls. Source snapshots, executable analyses, machine-readable outputs and supplementary methods accompany the draft package. These materials permit careful inspection of the precise observations and their limits without requiring the reader to accept a discovery narrative. Editorial assessment of scientific validity and distinct contribution remains necessary.
